Common questions.

If you need anything else, email peter@moversync.co.uk.

Can it receive leads from our current providers?

Yes. MoverSync parses structured emails from providers like Getamover, Pinlocal, and Compare My Move, mapping them directly to a clean customer record.

Does the demo save real customer data?

No. The public demo uses seeded example data in your browser. Refreshing the demo restores the default sample workspace.

Can our process be customised?

Yes. Statuses, templates, user roles, reminders, quote rules, and reports can be configured around your exact operating process.

Is there a long contract?

No. All plans are billed monthly. Any custom integration or migration work is agreed separately before work begins.
